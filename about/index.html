02 /PROFESSIONAL STORY

From screens
to the systems
behind them.

My start in interface design was at Reddway.in, working on seller dashboards and order fulfilment. At Permute Solutions, reusable components and workflow prototypes expanded that foundation into platforms.

At Khushi Baby, the context changed: multiple roles, field constraints, and information moving between teams. Work across CHIP-2, Samasta, and CHO Soft brought me closer to the connections between a screen, a workflow, and the wider service.

I’m drawn to problems where complexity has a practical consequence: a form that is hard to complete, a task without an obvious owner, or a state that leaves someone guessing. My direction as a Product Designer is to connect those everyday details with broader product decisions.
