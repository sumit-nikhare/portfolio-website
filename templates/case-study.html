01 / THE SHORT VERSION

One idea.
A considered direction.

Give a small team one calm place to understand its work, pick a priority, and move forward. This is an illustrative design exercise, not a commissioned or user-validated product.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

When everything is urgent,
nothing feels clear.

Workspaces can ask people to process too much at once: deadlines, messages, projects, and competing priorities. This concept starts with a simple question: what if the next useful action was the easiest thing to find?

03 / WHAT GUIDED THE DESIGN

05 / BRINGING IT TOGETHER

A workspace that gives
your attention back.

The overview balances a short summary, a set of active projects, and one contextual prompt. A warm background and selective lime accents establish an inviting hierarchy without turning the interface into a collection of alerts.
