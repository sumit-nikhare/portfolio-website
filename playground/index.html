EXPERIMENT 002 / TYPOGRAPHY

Words with personality.

A variable font, a few controls, and a surprising amount of expression. Try your own words.

What if?

Idea
Use the same words to explore a different tone.
Experiment
Edit the phrase, then change the font weight and letter spacing. Compare a light, open setting with a heavier, tighter one.
Result
A live type specimen changes without switching fonts. This demonstrates expression, not a tested improvement in readability.

Idea

Experiment

One component supports two densities and an explicit saved state. The collection is a local demonstration; nothing is stored.

EXPERIMENT 004 / SVG MOTION

A line of curiosity.

One path. A little timing. Follow the line as it traces its way through an abstract landscape.

CURIOSITY RARELY TRAVELS IN A STRAIGHT LINE.

Reduced motion shows the completed path.

Idea
Use movement to explain where a line leads.
Experiment
Play the path, pause partway, then resume or replay. The moving point keeps its place along the same curve.
Result
Timing turns a static line into a sequence. Reduced motion keeps the complete drawing visible without requiring playback.
