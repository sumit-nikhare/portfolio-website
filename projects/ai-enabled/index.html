01 / THE SHORT VERSION

Useful work.
A clear contribution.

An integrated dashboard experience translating AI model outputs into reviewable information and human-led actions.

I led the dashboard UX for human-in-the-loop review, defining explainability cues, confidence states, review actions, and escalation paths around AI-enabled insights.

This case summary is based on my résumé. The diagrams were recreated for this portfolio to explain the workflow; they are not production screenshots or archived research artifacts.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Make the work
easier to understand.

Model output needs context before someone can use it responsibly. A dashboard must explain what an insight means, how certain it is, and what a reviewer can do next.

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Structure before
surface detail.

01

Add context to the output

Make room for explainability cues alongside an insight.

02

Keep people in the loop

Provide review actions instead of treating the model output as a final decision.

03

Make uncertainty actionable

Connect confidence states with appropriate review or escalation paths.
