01 / THE SHORT VERSION

Useful work.
A clear contribution.

Primary healthcare application design across registration, outpatient services, records, inventory, and related clinical-service workflows.

I designed application screens across OPD, registration, inventory, NCDs, maternal health, and family planning, using simplification, defaults, and validation guardrails to clarify critical flows.

This case summary is based on my résumé. The diagrams were recreated for this portfolio to explain the workflow; they are not production screenshots or archived research artifacts.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Make the work
easier to understand.

Facility teams move between registration, consultations, records, and supplies throughout the day. Repeated steps and form-heavy interactions add friction to work that already demands attention.

03 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Structure before
surface detail.

01

Keep the task connected

Organize registration, service selection, and completion as a coherent workflow.

02

Reduce repeat effort

Use considered defaults and a clearer sequence to remove unnecessary steps in critical tasks.

03

Support accurate entry

Make required information, validation, and completion states understandable before handoff.
